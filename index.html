What is live anime music?

It's the music of anime, Japanese games, and J-Pop/J-Rock — anisong, theme songs, and soundtracks — played live by real bands: rock bands, jazz and fusion combos, pianists, and chamber players, instead of a DJ track or a recording.

At a BAAM show that can mean a full band concert, an open jam where anyone in the audience can sit in, live band karaoke, or dueling pianos taking requests. You don't need to know the series to enjoy it — if you like live music, you already get it.

Concerts and events for this music are few and far between outside of conventions — which is exactly why BAAM puts on its own.

Community

Discord is home

BAAM started as a way for anime bands and musicians to form a network — and the network lives in the Discord server. Instagram (@bayareaanimemusic) is where shows are announced; the server is where the community actually happens.

Bands form here. Many anime bands have formed inside the server — musicians from beginner to professional, playing anime music and genres well beyond it. If you're looking for a band, a collaborator, or a fill-in, this is where to start.

Planning happens here. Events, jam sessions, and collaborations are planned with the community, and members share photos and clips from shows.

Playing happens here, too. Members host informal jams at their houses and venues around the Bay, and chamber music sessions among members — when one grows a date, a venue, and an audience, it becomes a listed event above.

The lead sheet library. The server holds a library of hundreds of lead sheets, contributed by members — some from the group behind VGLS — shared with members through the server. It's how a jam with strangers can still play the song: the charts are there.

Who it's for. Musicians, bands, fans, cosplayers, and photographers — you don't have to perform, and you don't have to be in a band. If you love anime and Japanese music in the Bay Area, you belong here.

Get Involved

Volunteer-run

BAAM is volunteer-run and community-based — the organizing, promotion, and day-of work behind every show is volunteer labor. There are four ways in:

Play a BAAM show

Bands and musicians in the network play BAAM concerts and events. Join the Discord, come to a jam, and get to know the community — that's how lineups come together.

Volunteer your time

Help run the shows: event-day help, promotion and flyering, and hosting jam sessions and chamber sessions. One show or ongoing — both matter.

Contribute

Lead sheets for the library, photography and video at shows, design, gear and backline, or a space to play in — in-kind contributions keep events possible.

Support BAAM

Come to shows, share photos and clips, and donate to support the mission. BAAM is a community nonprofit, and typically takes no cut from musicians except to cover venue and event expense costs.

The way to start any of these is the same: join the Discord and say hello.

About BAAM

Bay Area Anime Music celebrates and shares Japanese anime music culture through live concerts and community events, building a network of local musicians.

The BAAM group started as a way for anime bands and musicians to form a network. Concerts and events are few and far between for anime fans outside of the occasional con, so it's difficult for anime musicians to build up credibility or find venues to perform at. BAAM works to resolve that by setting up its own events and concerts to help build a strong community of anime music fans, and helping connect musicians together for collaboration.

Our musicians tend to focus on live instrumental music playing J-Rock and J-Pop — but who knows what the future holds?

BAAM is a California nonprofit public benefit corporation based in the Bay Area, incorporated July 21, 2025.

What BAAM does

Ticketed and community concerts · open jam sessions · live band karaoke · dueling pianos · con panels, meetups, and busking · a Discord community where bands form and a member-built lead sheet library lives.
